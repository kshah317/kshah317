## Background

I spent years in economics and public policy, including a Master's in Political Economy of South Asia at Oxford and an MiM at LSE, followed by some years as a Project Manager at a public sector institution running large-scale e-governance programs and digital public infrastructure (DPI) initaitives. My work involved an ML-driven tax net broadening exercise on 20M+ households, a refugee registration project for UNHCR and managing a migration management system in partnership with UN-IOM and EU Frontex. My expereince has always indicated that effective policy and public benefit outcomes depend largely on the underlying data infrastructure and models, which is what pulled me towards CS. I'm now doing an MS in Computer Science/AI at Penn Engineering, while consulting on Enterprise AI, AI Governance and Ethics, in an attempt to build the technical depth that adds to my domain expertise.
